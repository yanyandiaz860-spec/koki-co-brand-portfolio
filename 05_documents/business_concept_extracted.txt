1. Brand Name / Business Name

kOKi.co

2. Brand Story

History
kOKi.co started in 2026 as a small rolling store selling only one cookie flavor. The idea started from a simple thought: cookies are a small treat, but they can easily make someone’s day better. At first, only a small batch was made and sold to friends and people they knew. As more customers started enjoying the cookies, the business slowly added new flavors and improved the recipes based on what people liked.

The name kOKi.co came from the idea of having a place where people can always find a cookie they’ll enjoy, no matter where they are. Instead of having customers look for a cookie shop, the business decided to bring the cookies closer to them through rolling stores and mobile vendors. What started as one small store and one cookie flavor eventually grew into a mobile cookie business reaching different areas of Nueva Ecija.

Vision
kOKi.co aims to become a well-known local cookie brand in Nueva Ecija, known for fresh, delicious, and affordable cookies that customers can easily enjoy wherever they are.

Mission
Our mission is to provide freshly baked, good-quality cookies at an affordable price. We also want to make our products more accessible by bringing our rolling stores and mobile vendors closer to schools, communities, events, and busy areas.

Unique Appeal
What makes kOKi.co different is that we don’t just wait for customers to come to us—we bring the cookies closer to them. Through our rolling stores and mobile vendors, customers can enjoy fresh cookies in different places around Nueva Ecija. With different flavors to choose from and prices that are easy on the budget, kOKi.co makes satisfying your cookie cravings simple, convenient, and enjoyable.

6. Competitor Analysis

KOKI Cookies

Strength: Cookie-focused business with different cookie flavors.

Weakness: Customers still need to go to their selling location.

kOKi.co’s Advantage: kOKi.co uses rolling stores and mobile vendors, so we can bring fresh cookies closer to customers.

Edna’s Cakeland

Strength: Known local bakery that offers different baked products and cookies.

Weakness: Cookies are only one of the many products they sell.

kOKi.co’s Advantage: kOKi.co focuses mainly on cookies and offers different flavors at affordable prices.

Julie’s Bakeshop

Strength: Accessible and known for affordable baked products.

Weakness: Cookies are not the main focus of the business.

kOKi.co’s Advantage: kOKi.co specializes in cookies and can bring them directly to different locations through its rolling store.

Cindy’s Bakery

Strength: Offers affordable cookies and other pastries.

Weakness: Has fewer cookie choices compared with a cookie-focused business.

kOKi.co’s Advantage: kOKi.co offers more cookie flavors and makes them more accessible through mobile selling.

Home-Based Cookie Sellers

Strength: Homemade cookies and customizable orders.

Weakness: Usually have limited locations and operating hours.

kOKi.co’s Advantage: kOKi.co can reach more customers through its rolling stores and mobile vendors.
